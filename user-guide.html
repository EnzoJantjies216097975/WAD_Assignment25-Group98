Getting Started

Welcome to the NUST Timetable Manager! This comprehensive guide will walk you through all the features and help you create your perfect academic schedule.

Tip: This guide is organized sequentially. If you're new to the system, we recommend reading from top to bottom.

Creating an Account

To start using the timetable manager, you'll need to create an account. Follow these simple steps:

  1. Navigate to Registration: Click on the "Login / Register" button in the navigation bar, then select the "Register" tab.
  2. Fill in Your Details:
    • Enter your full name as it appears in NUST records
    • Provide your 9-digit student number
    • Use your NUST email address (e.g., youremail@nust.na)
    • Select your program from the dropdown menu
  3. Create a Strong Password: Your password must be at least 8 characters long. Use a combination of letters, numbers, and symbols for better security.
  4. Agree to Terms: Read and accept the terms and conditions by checking the box.
  5. Submit: Click the "Create Account" button. You'll be automatically logged in and redirected to your dashboard.

Logging In

Once you have an account, logging in is quick and easy:

  1. Click "Login / Register" in the navigation bar
  2. Ensure you're on the "Login" tab
  3. Enter your NUST email address
  4. Type your password (click the eye icon to show/hide it)
  5. Optionally, check "Remember me" to stay logged in
  6. Click the "Login" button
Security Tip: Never share your password with anyone. If you suspect your account has been compromised, change your password immediately in the Profile Settings.

Dashboard Overview

After logging in, you'll be taken to your dashboard. This is your central hub for managing timetables. The dashboard includes:

My Schedules

View all your saved timetables, create new ones, or manage existing schedules.

Course Browser

Search and explore all available NUST courses with detailed information.

Profile Settings

Update your personal information and account settings.

Share/Export

Generate shareable links or export your timetable as PDF or image.

Creating Your First Schedule

Creating a timetable is intuitive with our drag-and-drop interface. Here's how:

Step 1: Start a New Schedule
  • From your dashboard, click "Create New Schedule"
  • Give your schedule a descriptive name (e.g., "Semester 2 2025")
  • Select the semester and year
Step 2: Understanding the Interface

The timetable editor consists of:

  • Grid View: Weekly calendar showing days (Monday-Friday) and time slots (08:00-17:00)
  • Course Sidebar: List of available courses that you can drag onto the grid
  • Toolbar: Quick actions like save, view options, and export
Step 3: Adding Courses
  1. Browse or search for a course in the sidebar
  2. Click and hold on a course card
  3. Drag the course to the appropriate time slot on the grid
  4. Release to drop the course into place
  5. The system will snap the course to the correct time alignment
Pro Tip: Valid drop zones will highlight in green as you drag. Red zones indicate conflicts or invalid placements.

Browsing and Searching Courses

The course browser makes it easy to find exactly what you need:

Search Options:
  • By Course Code: Enter codes like "WAD621S" or "PRG510S"
  • By Course Name: Search for "Web Application" or "Programming"
  • By Department: Filter by Computer Science, Cyber Security, etc.
Filter Options:
  • Year Level (1st, 2nd, 3rd year)
  • Semester (1 or 2)
  • Credit Value
  • Lecturer
Course Details Modal:

Click on any course card to view detailed information including lecturer names, venue assignments, prerequisites, credit hours, and course description.

Managing Schedule Conflicts

The system automatically detects three types of conflicts:

Time Overlap Conflicts

When two courses have overlapping time slots. The system will prevent you from placing a course if it conflicts with an existing one.

Resolution: Choose a different section or time slot for one of the courses.
Venue Conflicts

When you're scheduled to be in two different locations at the same time or have insufficient travel time between venues.

Resolution: Adjust one of the courses to allow adequate travel time.
Prerequisite Warnings

When you attempt to add a course without having completed its prerequisites.

Resolution: This is a warning, not a hard block. Review the prerequisites and confirm if you've met them.

Saving Your Schedule

Your schedule is saved in two ways:

1. Auto-Save (Local Storage)
  • Changes are automatically saved to your browser every few seconds
  • Works offline - no internet connection needed
  • Data persists even if you close the browser
  • Limited to the current device
2. Cloud Save (Database)
  • Click the "Save to Cloud" button in the toolbar
  • Requires internet connection
  • Synchronized across all your devices
  • Permanent storage on NUST servers
  • Can create multiple schedule versions
Best Practice: Use cloud save for your final schedules and local storage for experimenting with different combinations.

Sharing and Exporting

Sharing with Classmates:
  1. Open the schedule you want to share
  2. Click "Share" in the toolbar
  3. Click "Generate Share Link"
  4. Copy the unique URL and send it to your classmates
  5. Shared schedules are view-only for recipients
Exporting as PDF:
  1. Navigate to "Share/Export" section
  2. Select "Export as PDF"
  3. Choose your preferred layout (portrait or landscape)
  4. Click "Generate PDF"
  5. Download and save or print your timetable
Exporting as Image:
  1. Go to "Share/Export"
  2. Select "Export as Image"
  3. Choose image format (PNG recommended for quality)
  4. Download the image file
  5. Perfect for sharing via messaging apps

Profile Settings

Manage your account and preferences from the Profile Settings page:

Account Information:
  • Update your name and contact details
  • Change your program if you switch majors
  • View your student number (cannot be changed)
Preferences:
  • Set default semester and year
  • Choose color scheme for course cards
  • Configure notification settings
  • Set time zone preferences
Change Password:
  1. Navigate to Profile Settings
  2. Click "Change Password"
  3. Enter your current password
  4. Type your new password twice
  5. Click "Update Password"
