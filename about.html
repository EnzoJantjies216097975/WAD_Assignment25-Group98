About Our Project

Transforming academic scheduling for NUST students

Introduction & Background

The NUST Timetable Manager is a comprehensive web-based application developed to help Namibia University of Science and Technology students with their scheduling issues.

Students now face more complicated scheduling issues as they navigate their new educational environment. Creating the perfect timetable has become a significant administrative burden for students due to the wide range of courses, elective options, campus locations, and competing time slots. Spreadsheets, paper planners, and screenshot collections are examples of manual procedures that many students now employ, which leads to inefficiencies, schedule conflicts, and lost possibilities for better time management.

The University Timetable Manager addresses this pressing need by providing a centralized, interactive web application that simplifies the whole scheduling procedure. These days, having a reliable digital solution for organizing timetables is crucial in our quickly digitizing educational world. By reducing the administrative load of scheduling, students can now focus more on their studies, since this project understands the apparent link between academic achievement and effective time management.

Problem Statement

There are currently very few integrated, user-friendly tools available to students for efficiently creating, visualizing, and managing their university timetables. The current manual methods are prone to mistakes, difficult to modify, and don't provide features for conflict detection or schedule optimization. In addition, students can't easily share timetables with their peers for coordination or save multiple schedule variations for comparison and cooperation.

Our Solution


The NUST University Timetable Manager is a fully functioning web application that transforms how students manage their academic timetables. The application features a visually appealing grid-based interface representing the weekly calendar, where each cell corresponds to a specific time slot and day combination.

Core Features and Functionality

Interactive Schedule Builder

The main component of the application is a drag-and-drop interface where students can browse available courses from a sidebar panel and drag them directly onto their timetable grid. Instant visual feedback is provided by the system, which also automatically avoids conflicts and identifies acceptable drop zones.

Intelligent Conflict Management

The application implements conflict detection that checks for time overlaps, venue conflicts, and prerequisites. When conflicts are detected, the system provides clear visual indicators and suggests alternative time slots or sections where available.

Multilayered Data Storage

The solution employs a dual storage strategy. Local Storage provides immediate, offline-capable storage for quick access and temporary saves. For permanent storage and cross-device synchronization, the backend utilizes PHP with MySQL database, implementing secure API endpoints for CRUD operations on schedule data.
