Quick Start Guide

Get up and running in minutes

1
Create Account

Register with your NUST email address and student number. Your account will be created instantly.

2
Browse Courses

Explore the course catalog using the search and filter features. Find courses by code, name, or department.

3
Build Schedule

Drag courses onto your timetable grid. The system will automatically check for conflicts and alert you.

4
Save & Share

Save your completed schedule and share it with classmates. Export to PDF or image format for offline use.
