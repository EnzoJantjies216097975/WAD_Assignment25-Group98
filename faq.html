Account & Login

Click on "Login / Register" in the navigation bar, select the "Register" tab, and fill in your details including your NUST email address, student number, and chosen password. Make sure to use your official NUST email (ending in @nust.na).
On the login page, click "Forgot Password?" below the password field. Enter your registered email address, and you'll receive instructions to reset your password. If you don't receive an email within 10 minutes, check your spam folder or contact support.
No, you must use your official NUST email address (@nust.na) to register. This ensures only NUST students can access the system and helps maintain data security and integrity.
After logging in, go to Profile Settings, click on "Change Password," enter your current password and your new password twice, then click "Update Password." For security reasons, you'll need to log in again after changing your password.

Creating Schedules

From your dashboard, click "Create New Schedule." Give your timetable a name, select the semester and year, then click "Create." You'll be taken to the timetable editor where you can start adding courses by dragging them from the sidebar onto the weekly grid.
Yes! You can create as many schedule versions as you like. This is useful for comparing different course combinations or planning for different scenarios. Each schedule is saved separately and can be easily accessed from your dashboard.
Go to "My Schedules" on your dashboard, find the schedule you want to delete, click the three-dot menu icon, and select "Delete." You'll be asked to confirm before the schedule is permanently removed. Note: This action cannot be undone.
Yes, your changes are automatically saved to local storage every few seconds. However, to sync your schedule across devices and ensure permanent cloud storage, click the "Save to Cloud" button. We recommend doing this regularly, especially after major changes.

Courses

Use the search bar in the Course Browser. You can search by course code (e.g., "WAD621S"), course name (e.g., "Web Application"), or lecturer name. You can also use filters to narrow down results by department, year level, or semester.
The course database is updated at the start of each semester with official NUST course offerings. If you can't find a course, it may not be offered in the current semester, or there may be a data synchronization delay. Contact support with the specific course code for assistance.
Click on any course card in the Course Browser to open a detailed modal window. This shows complete information including course description, credit hours, prerequisites, lecturer(s), venue assignment, and scheduled time slots.

Handling Conflicts

A scheduling conflict occurs when two courses overlap in time, making it impossible to attend both. The system detects three types: time overlaps, venue conflicts (being in two places at once), and prerequisite warnings (missing required prior courses).
You have several options: choose a different section of one course (if available), select an alternative time slot, or replace one of the conflicting courses with a different course that fits your schedule. The system will suggest alternatives when possible.
Prerequisite warnings are informational only - the system won't prevent you from adding the course to your timetable. However, you should verify with your academic advisor whether you've met the requirements or can get special permission to take the course.

Sharing & Export

Open the schedule you want to share, click "Share" in the toolbar, then "Generate Share Link." Copy the unique URL and send it to your classmates via email, messaging, or social media. The link provides view-only access - they can't edit your schedule.
No, shared links are read-only. Recipients can view your timetable but cannot make any changes. Your schedule remains secure and only you can edit it when logged into your account.
PDF format is better for printing and maintains high quality at any size. It's ideal for official documentation. Image format (PNG/JPG) is better for quick sharing on social media or messaging apps, and has smaller file sizes. Choose based on your intended use.

Technical Issues

The timetable manager works best on modern browsers: Google Chrome (version 90+), Mozilla Firefox (version 88+), Microsoft Edge (version 90+), and Safari (version 14+). For the best experience, keep your browser updated to the latest version.
Yes! The application is fully responsive and optimized for mobile devices. You can create and edit timetables on smartphones and tablets. The mobile interface uses touch-friendly controls instead of drag-and-drop.
Don't worry! Your changes are automatically saved to local storage on your device, so you won't lose any work. When your connection is restored, click "Save to Cloud" to sync your changes to the server.
Try these solutions: 1) Refresh the page (F5 or Cmd+R), 2) Clear your browser cache, 3) Check if JavaScript is enabled in your browser settings, 4) Try a different browser, or 5) Disable browser extensions that might interfere. If problems persist, contact support.
