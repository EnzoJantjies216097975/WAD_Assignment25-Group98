Core Features

The essential tools for efficient timetable management

Interactive Drag & Drop

Intuitively build your timetable by dragging courses from the course catalog directly onto your weekly schedule grid. The interface provides instant visual feedback, showing you exactly where courses can be placed and highlighting valid drop zones as you drag.

Intelligent Conflict Detection

The system automatically detects and prevents scheduling conflicts before they happen. It checks for time overlaps, venue conflicts, and prerequisite requirements, alerting you immediately with clear visual indicators and helpful suggestions for alternative time slots.

Cloud Synchronization

Your timetable data is securely stored in the cloud using MySQL database, allowing you to access your schedules from any device. The system also uses local storage for offline access, so you can view and edit your timetable even without an internet connection.

Course Browser

Browse through the complete NUST course catalog with powerful search and filter capabilities. Search by course code, name, or department. Filter courses by year level, semester, or credits. Each course displays detailed information including lecturer names, venues, and time slots.

Multiple Schedules

Create and manage multiple versions of your timetable to compare different scheduling scenarios. This is perfect for exploring various course combinations or planning for different semesters. You can easily switch between saved schedules and delete versions you no longer need.

Schedule Sharing

Share your timetable with classmates using unique shareable links. This makes it easy to coordinate study groups, compare schedules with friends, or share your plan with academic advisors. The shared schedules are view-only to protect your data.

Export Functionality

Export your completed timetable in multiple formats for offline use. Generate a PDF document for printing or save your schedule as an image file that you can easily share via email or messaging apps. Perfect for having a physical copy of your schedule.

Responsive Design

The application is fully responsive and works seamlessly across all devices. Whether you're using a desktop computer, laptop, tablet, or smartphone, you'll have a consistent and optimized experience. The mobile interface is specifically designed for touch interactions.

User Profile Management

Create and customize your personal profile with your student information. Manage your account settings, update your preferences, and securely change your password. Your profile helps personalize the experience and keeps your timetable data organized.

Technical Highlights

Backend Technologies

  • PHP 7.4+ for server-side processing
  • MySQL for relational database management

  • JSON for data exchange format
  • Session management for authentication
